# M17 — Rate limiting & lockout, account deletion, security regression (fast track: P2-31, P2-32, P2-35, P3-22 part)

Found while planning: `@RateLimited` was only a marker — **nothing enforced it** (no brute-force protection on sign-in, recovery or booking). This milestone closes that gap first.

## Decisions (engineering defaults — flagged)

- **Request limits** (global guard, per instance, in memory, sliding one-minute window, `429 RATE_LIMITED` + `Retry-After`): routes marked `auth` — per address (default 60/min) and per account name in the body (10/min, so one victim's mailbox cannot be flooded with recovery mails from many addresses); `booking` — per signed-in customer (30/min), not per address (customers behind one NAT are not punished). Health/catalogue routes are unmarked and never throttled. `RATE_LIMIT_ENABLED`, `AUTH_RATE_PER_IP`, `AUTH_RATE_PER_ACCOUNT`, `BOOKING_RATE_PER_USER` in the environment. A shared store (Postgres/Redis) is the later step when several instances run; only `RateLimitService` changes.
- **Sign-in lockout**: 5 wrong passwords from one address for one account lock that **pair** for 15 minutes (the real owner on another address is unaffected — a stranger cannot lock someone out of their own account); each repeat is twice as long (up to 8×) until a successful sign-in; while locked even the right password is refused (no oracle for guessers). Failures spread across many addresses lock the **account** itself at a higher bar (25). Unknown accounts are throttled identically (no enumeration). Covers `/v1/auth/login` and the browser-session login. Password reset is unaffected, so a locked-out owner can still recover.
- **Account deletion** `DELETE /v1/me` (+ `GET /v1/me`): needs the password (accounts with one) or a recent sign-in (provider-only accounts); refused while a booking is live (`HAS_FUTURE_BOOKINGS`) and for staff; removes email/phone/name, every sign-in method, verification tokens and the Aadhaar record (the card can be used again), revokes every session; **keeps** bookings, payments and refunds (the money trail) without the person's details; audited; the email address becomes free to register again. App: "Delete my account" with a password dialog and plain explanations.
- **Security regression suite** (P2-35, part of `test:api`): another customer's booking id on every booking route answers exactly like a missing one (404); forged/tampered tokens (alg none, changed payload, foreign key, wrong audience/issuer, expired, garbage, truncated) are 401 on customer and admin routes; a customer token on every admin route (GET and POST) is 403 and anonymous 401; a suspended customer cannot book with a still-valid token.
