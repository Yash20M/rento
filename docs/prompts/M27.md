# M27 — Android back button and a remembered search place (P3-49, P3-50)

**Goal:** the phone's back button behaves like back inside the app, and the app reopens where the customer last searched — without keeping a precise position on the phone. Both found by the owner on the live demo (2026-10-23).

**Scope**
- **P3-49 back button.** Reproduced on the emulator: on the *My bookings* / *Account* tab, back closes the app; a screen opened by replacing the history (`context.go`: checkout after reserving, the booking after paying, a deep link) has nothing under it, so back closes the app too.
  - `HomeShell`: back on any tab but Explore switches to Explore; back on Explore leaves the app (Android convention).
  - Every signed-in page outside the shell (drawer, item, checkout, booking, KYC, sign-in methods, verify email) gets one shared wrapper: when nothing is under it, back goes to Explore instead of closing the app; when something is under it, back pops as before (predictive-back animation kept). Sign-in screens are unchanged (back leaves the app there).
- **P3-50 remembered place** (owner decision 2026-10-23, ADR-016, amends README §10.6). The choice stays exact **in memory** for this run. A copy rounded to **2 decimals (~1.1 km)** — plus the name for a named demo place — is written to secure storage under `last_place` and restored at the next start (Explore searches straight away). Wiped whenever the session ends (sign-out, account deletion, refused session) together with the saved bookings, and the in-memory choice is forgotten at sign-out. A corrupt or out-of-range saved value is ignored.

**Tests (written first, `test/features/navigation_test.dart`)**
- N-01 back on *My bookings* ⇒ Explore, the app stays open · N-02 back on Explore ⇒ the app asks the system to close · N-03 drawer opened from Explore ⇒ back returns to Explore · N-04 after reserving (checkout replaced the history) back ⇒ Explore, app open · N-05 the booking opened after paying ⇒ back ⇒ Explore.
- L-01 choosing GPS 18.53412, 73.89417 saves exactly `18.53` / `73.89` (no more decimals) · L-02 a saved place is searched at start with the saved coordinates and no "Where are you?" prompt · L-03 a named demo place comes back with its name · L-04 sign-out deletes `last_place`, and the next sign-in starts at "Where are you?" · L-05 a corrupt or out-of-range saved value is ignored.

**Rules:** no new dependency; no coordinates in logs or URLs; mutation check on the rounding (save unrounded ⇒ L-01 fails) and on the wipe (skip delete ⇒ L-04 fails).

**Done when:** new tests green, Flutter suite green, `pnpm check`, `mobile:analyze|test|format:check|codegen:check` green, full `pnpm verify` green, APK rebuilt and the two behaviours seen on the emulator, ADR-016 + README §10.6 + plan updated.
