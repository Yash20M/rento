## Decisions (engineering defaults — flagged)

- **Reassign** (`POST /v1/admin/bookings/{id}/reassign`, `GET …/reassign-options`; `bookings:write`, drawer-scoped): only for `confirmed`/`held` bookings, same drawer and model, target unit `available` and free for exactly the booking's window (+ buffer). One transaction releases the old occupancy and inserts the new one; the exclusion constraint decides, a refusal (`BOOKING_SLOT_TAKEN`) rolls the release back — the booking is left exactly as it was. Time and price never change. Reason mandatory, audited (`booking.reassigned`), a history entry is added.
- **Maintenance blocks** (`GET/POST /v1/admin/assets/{id}/blocks`, `DELETE …/blocks/{blockId}`, `GET …/impact-preview`): `assets:write` to block/lift, `bookings:read` for the preview (it shows customers). A block is an `asset_occupancies` row (`maintenance` | `admin_block`, migration 0013 adds `reason`, `created_by`). **Nothing is ever cancelled or moved silently**: a block over bookings or another block is refused with `409 HAS_FUTURE_BOOKINGS` naming them (no customer details in the error); the constraint still has the last word if a customer books between check and insert. Holds that already ran out are expired first so they do not block. Past blocks, reversed times, > 366 days, reason < 3 characters are refused.
- **Schedule** (`GET /v1/admin/drawers/{id}/schedule`, `bookings:read`, drawer-scoped, ≤ 31 days): every unit with its bookings (blocked time includes the cleaning buffer) and blocks; times shown in the drawer's time zone.
- **Reports** (`reports:read`): bookings CONFIRMED in a window (counted once, on the day they were paid, in the drawer's time zone); money is the cash view — taken (rental + deposit + extensions) minus refunded/pending refunds — and the screen says it is not profit. **Payments ledger** (`GET /v1/admin/payments`). **Settings** (`settings:read`): read-only view of the rules in force (they come from the deployment environment; no editable settings store — P2-19 stays open), no secrets, a demo-payments notice.
- Admin screens: Schedule (timeline per unit, block dialog with live impact preview, lift block), booking modal "Move to another unit…", Reports, Payments, Settings; menu entries follow permissions.
- Cut (follow-up tasks): asset **transfers** between drawers (P2-40), customer suspension, editable settings (P2-19), CSV export, deep links from the report to bookings.
