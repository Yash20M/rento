## Decisions (engineering defaults — flagged)

- **Transfer**: start → the asset is `in_transfer` with a `transfer` occupancy (migration 0014 `asset_transfers`); complete → it belongs to the destination drawer and is `available`; cancel → unchanged. Refused with `HAS_FUTURE_BOOKINGS` while the asset has upcoming bookings or blocks (staff resolve those first). Starting needs `assets:transfer` on both drawers; completing needs it on the destination.
- **Suspension**: `users.status = suspended` (column existed). Revokes all refresh tokens, blocks sign-in and new bookings; existing bookings stay for staff to handle; staff accounts are refused (manage them under Staff). Unrestricted `users:write` only, reason mandatory, audited.
- **Reminders**: `REMINDER_MINUTES` (default 60; 0 = off); one email per confirmed booking (`bookings.reminder_sent_at`), through the outbox. Push and per-customer preferences stay open (need FCM keys / a product decision).
- Suspended-booking refusal reuses `FORBIDDEN` (no new error code).
